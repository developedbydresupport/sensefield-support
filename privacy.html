1. Scope and contact

This policy describes the Sensefield iPhone app, this support website, and communications you choose to send to the app publisher. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the publisher of Sensefield. For privacy questions, contact developedbydresupport@gmail.com.

Sensefield has no user accounts, advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking profiles.

2. Information used by the app

Depending on the instrument you open, Sensefield accesses the following information through Apple’s device frameworks. Processing takes place on your iPhone to display and analyze readings.

The app requests camera, microphone, motion or location permission when needed. You may decline or revoke a permission; the features that depend on it may be unavailable. Sensefield does not save raw photos, video, audio recordings or room-mesh files.

3. Fusion sessions and optional exports

Opening Fusion starts a session that holds derived numerical sensor measurements in memory. A session records up to approximately 30 minutes of metrics. Selecting Prepare CSV creates a file in the app’s temporary storage. Selecting the save or share action lets you choose where that file goes.

A Fusion CSV can contain precise latitude and longitude, heading, timestamps and sensor readings. It contains derived metrics rather than raw camera images or microphone recordings. Review the file before sharing it.

Sensefield does not automatically upload exports to us. If you choose email, cloud storage or another app as the destination, that service receives the file and handles it under its own privacy practices.

4. Retention and your controls

Live analysis, histories and Fusion session metrics are held in app memory. Stopping Fusion preserves that session’s metrics for export while the session remains available. Starting a new Fusion session clears the prior in-memory recording. The app does not provide a persistent session library.

Temporary CSV files are not immediately deleted by the app after you share or leave a session. They can remain in the app’s temporary storage until iOS removes them or the app’s data is deleted. Deleting Sensefield from your iPhone removes its local app data. Copies you saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately.

You can review and revoke permissions in iPhone Settings. To stop a module’s sensor use, leave that instrument; Fusion also has a Stop control. The app has no server account or server-side sensor history for us to access or delete.

5. Sharing, advertising and tracking

Sensefield has no developer-operated backend, automatic telemetry uploads, advertising network or cross-app tracking. We do not receive, sell or rent sensor data from your use of the app. Sensor information reaches us only if you choose to include it in a support message or other communication.

Apple provides the operating system, permission controls and services such as Location Services. Those services are subject to Apple’s own privacy practices. Destinations you choose for exported files are separate from Sensefield.

6. Information you send to support

If you email support, we receive your email address and any name, message, device details, screenshots or files you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request, troubleshoot the app, address privacy or security concerns, and maintain necessary support records. Support messages are handled using Gmail and may be processed by Google as the email service provider.

Please send only information needed to explain the issue. Avoid sending raw recordings, private images, precise location or an unedited Fusion CSV unless it is necessary and you want to share it with us.

We retain support communications only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support or security records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete a support message using the contact address below. We do not use support communications for advertising or sell them.

7. This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not use analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by us, or web forms. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser information, to operate and protect its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email links open your email application; a message is sent only when you choose to send it. Your email provider also handles the communication. See Google’s privacy policy for information about the Gmail service.

8. Privacy questions and requests

For questions or requests concerning information you sent to us, email developedbydresupport@gmail.com with the subject “Sensefield privacy request.” Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to certain uses. We will respond as required by applicable law. Because app sensor processing stays on your device, we cannot retrieve that information from a server.

We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If a support message includes a child’s personal information, a parent or guardian may contact us to request its removal.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Sensefield or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Materially different data practices will be disclosed before they take effect where required.
