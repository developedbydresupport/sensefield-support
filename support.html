01 / Before you begin

Device and permissions.

Sensefield requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Depth maps, point clouds and room meshes require a LiDAR-equipped iPhone Pro with the relevant ARKit support. Other tools depend on the sensors available on your device.

Allow camera, microphone, motion and location access when a feature asks. If you declined, find Sensefield in iPhone Settings or review Privacy & Security to change access. You can use the remaining tools without granting every permission.

02 / A useful bug report

Help us reproduce it.

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Sensefield version and the instrument you opened.
  • What you expected, what happened and the steps that led to it.
  • Whether the relevant permissions were allowed.

A screenshot is optional. Remove private details before sending it. Please do not attach a Fusion CSV with coordinates unless the file is necessary to explain the issue and you want to share it.

Common questions.

The camera view or depth display is unavailable.

Check camera permission and confirm that the phone supports the selected feature. Leave the instrument and open it again. For LiDAR views, try a well-lit area, move slowly and point at textured, nearby surfaces. Very reflective, transparent or distant surfaces may produce unreliable depth.

A device without LiDAR cannot provide a LiDAR depth map or full Fusion spatial data. The iOS Simulator cannot supply real sensor readings for these features.

Sound is silent, or the channel comparison is unavailable.

Check microphone permission and the active audio route. iOS may provide a single input channel; channel comparison is then unavailable. Sound levels are displayed in dBFS and are not calibrated sound-pressure measurements.

Optional monitored playback uses the device’s audio output and can feed back into the microphone. Keep the volume low and turn monitoring off if you hear feedback.

Motion, heading, pressure or location is missing.

Review motion and location permissions and iPhone Location Services. Sensor support varies by device. Magnetic readings can change near metal and magnets. GPS accuracy can be limited indoors; try an open outdoor area and wait for the path to settle.

Atmospheric trend needs at least 20 seconds of readings. Relative altitude starts from a session reference and is not an absolute elevation measurement.

How do I save a Fusion session?

Open Fusion to begin a session, add markers if useful, and stop when finished. Choose Prepare CSV, then Save or share Fusion CSV to choose a destination. Save before leaving the session; Sensefield does not provide a persistent session library.

CSV files contain derived measurements, timestamps and, when available, precise coordinates and heading. They do not contain raw photos or audio recordings. Review location columns before sharing. A session is limited to approximately 30 minutes.

Why do a reading or overlay appear to move?

Phone movement, lighting changes, nearby magnetic objects, microphone noise and sensor timing affect readings. Camera enhancement cannot recover detail absent from a frame. The camera view uses crop magnification rather than a 48 MP still image. Display roll correction is limited and does not cancel translation.

Use Sensefield for exploration and approximate analysis. It is not a calibrated medical, emergency, safety or professional surveying instrument.

How do I reduce the visual motion?

Enable Reduce Motion in iPhone Accessibility settings. Sensefield then pauses its continuously animated artwork and shortens the opening reveal. You can also skip the opening animation.

Does Sensefield upload recordings or require an account?

No account is required. Sensor analysis takes place on your iPhone. The app has no automatic sensor-data uploads, ads or third-party analytics. An export is saved or shared only when you choose a destination. Read the privacy policy for details about temporary CSV files, support emails and website hosting.

Privacy when asking for help.

You choose what you send us. Crop or redact screenshots and remove location columns from sample exports whenever they are not relevant. Our privacy policy explains how support messages are handled.
